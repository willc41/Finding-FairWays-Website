Our Mission

Mentorship First, Golf Second

Finding FairWays is a student-led golf mentorship program that provides underprivileged middle school students with direct access to the sport. Through our relationship with a local golf course, we offer equipment, instruction, and regular course access at no cost.

High school students lead sessions focused on basic skills, a supportive environment, and making the game enjoyable for new players — opening the door for students who otherwise wouldn't have the chance to play.

The MSF Grant

A First-of-Its-Kind Golf Simulator

In 2025, we were approved for the largest grant in Mamaroneck Schools Foundation history, building a two-bay golf simulator at the Post Road gym that now serves the whole athletic program year-round.
